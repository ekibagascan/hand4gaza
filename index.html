The program

Technology as tenderness.

Thousands of children in Gaza are growing up with missing limbs. Clinics are scarce. Waiting lists are long. A bionic arm is not a gadget — it is a pencil, a handshake, a bicycle grip, a way back into the ordinary miracles of childhood.

Hand4Gaza funds lightweight myoelectric arms, clinical fittings, and a year of follow-up care. We work toward a future that looks like the one in our banner: bright, rooted, and still Palestinian.

Program initiated by Eki Bagas (@ekibagasc9i).

Pediatric first Repairable hardware Local training

What one kit holds

  • Custom-fitted myoelectric hand & socket
  • Sensors, charger, spare parts, and tools
  • Clinical fitting with a prosthetist
  • Twelve months of rehab check-ins
  • Training for a caregiver or local technician

Every kit is tagged, tracked, and reported through Kitabisa.

How hope moves

Four beats. One rising line.

01

Listen

Medical partners identify children who can use a myoelectric arm — age, residual limb, and daily life first.

02

Craft

Each socket is fitted. Joints, sensors, and a child-scale gripper are assembled for growth, not just for show.

03

Fit

Clinicians and families learn the arm together. Play is the first protocol. Writing comes next.

04

Stay

Parts, repairs, and check-ins continue for a year so the gift does not stall when a child grows.
